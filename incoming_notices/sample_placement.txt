The Training and Placement Cell is pleased to announce an on-campus recruitment
drive for the role of Graduate Software Engineer. The selection process consists
of an online aptitude and coding assessment, two technical interviews and an HR
discussion.

Eligible final-year students must register on the placement portal before
8 November 2026. Late registrations will not be accepted. Students who register
are expected to attend every round; absence without prior intimation will be
treated as per the placement policy.
