The Robotics and Automation Club invites students of all years to a one-day
hands-on workshop on building line-following robots. Participants will assemble a
chassis, wire infrared sensors and program a microcontroller during the session.

Kits will be provided. Seats are limited to forty participants and will be
allotted in order of registration. Participation certificates will be issued to
everyone who completes the workshop.
